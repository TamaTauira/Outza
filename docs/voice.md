# VibePeep voice

VibePeep is the friend who always knows what's happening: curious, fun, and socially connected, the local insider you text before you leave the house.

Voice: playful, curious, social, energetic, approachable, urban, spontaneous.

Usability comes first. A label a person has to scan in a bar, a button, or an error must stay short and say what will happen. Personality lives in headings, empty states, and mail. It does not replace the feature name.

## How to write

- Buttons are Title Case. Everything else is sentence case.
- Say **Tap**, never **Click**, when you mean the action. Analytics tables may still say **Clicks** or **ticket clicks**: that is the measured count, not an instruction.
- One name per feature. Use the table below. Do not invent a second nickname in a menu, toast, or email.
- Keep the tagline **Take a peep at what's happening.** with the lockup. Sub-line: **Events · Experiences · Local discoveries.**
- Write **LGBTIQ+**. Write **Sponsored** on paid placements. Write **Back to events** when leaving a side page for the board.
- Wit is welcome. Do not invent sell-outs, popularity, awards, prices, or "verified". Do not borrow slang from First Nations cultures.
- Legal pages, cookie and consent text, invoices, payment consent, and the Acknowledgement of Country stay plain. Do not restyle them to be jokey.
- Never put the old brand name in user-facing text. Hostnames that already exist are not a new brand mention.

## Names

| Feature | Say this | Do not also say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What's-on list | Board | feed, catalogue (in nav) |
| Map | Map | events map |
| Community listings | **Local** in the nav. **VibePeep Local** in headings and sentences | neighbourhood board |
| Saved events | **Saved** on the nav and the Save button. The page heading is **The keepers.** | favourites, bookmarks, watchlist |
| Account hub for saves, follows, reminders, calendar | My VibePeep | watchlist |
| Your own stops | Itinerary | Plan, My Itinerary, game plan |
| Suggested days and nights | Day and night plans | outing (as the product name), Plan (as a nav label) |
| Plain-language search | Ask VibePeep | AI search, the planner |
| Shared shortlist | VibePeep Together | Plan Together |
| Organiser home | Promoter dashboard | your dashboard |
| Area and venue figures | VibePeep Intelligence | intel |
| Mail editions | The Weekender, The Daily Drop, The Big Month | newsletter, digest (as the product name) |

**Saved** and **Itinerary** are different. **My VibePeep** holds the same saves plus follows, reminders, and the calendar file. **Day and night plans** are ready-made ideas. **VibePeep Together** is the shared vote with friends.

Ask VibePeep has two modes in the same bar: **Ask VibePeep** and **Day and night plans**. The submit control uses that name (**Ask** / **Plans** when the bar is narrow).

## Errors, empty states, forms

Say what is wrong and the next tap, in one or two short sentences. Drop "Please" when the sentence is already a clear instruction.

Good: "Give it a title, at least 5 characters."
Avoid: "Please enter a valid title in the field provided."

Empty states name the feature, then invite the next move. "No keepers yet" plus "Browse events" is enough.

## Mail and prompts

Watchlist mail is **My VibePeep** mail. Subjects stay specific ("A saved event is coming up", "From My VibePeep").

Editorial prompts (The Weekender and friends) use this voice and only the facts supplied. They never invent performers, prices, demand, or sell-outs.

Ask VibePeep's filter prompt returns JSON only. The voice line tells the model who it is; it must not chat.

Listing rewrites that are checked against the source stay fact-locked. Do not ask them to add words that are not already in the listing.

## Mascot

Dance names people see are the labels in `lib/mascot-dances.ts`. They stay separate from the animation files. Labels are short, playful, and do not name a real person or a trademarked routine. Reaction and roam lines are the same friend, not a commentator.
